# Capacitors in Parallel

## Necessary Definitions and Formulas

### 1. Equivalent Capacitance (Parallel)
When capacitors are connected in parallel, their equivalent (total) capacitance is simply the sum of their individual capacitances:

$$
C_{eq} = C_1 + C_2
$$

### 2. Charge Stored in a Capacitor ($Q$)
The amount of electric charge stored in a capacitor is the product of its capacitance and the voltage applied across it:

$$
Q = C \cdot V
$$

### 3. Energy Stored in a Capacitor ($W$)
The electrical potential energy stored in a capacitor can be calculated using its capacitance and the applied voltage:

$$
W = \frac{1}{2} C \cdot V^2
$$

*(Note: We use $W$ for energy to avoid confusion with Voltage, often denoted as $V$ or $U$)*

## Step-by-Step Solution

Let's identify the known variables from the problem statement:
* **Capacitance 1 ($C_1$):** $4\ \mu\text{F}$
* **Capacitance 2 ($C_2$):** $6\ \mu\text{F}$
* **Voltage ($V$):** $10\ \text{V}$

### Step 1: Calculate the Equivalent Capacitance ($C_{eq}$)
Because the capacitors are connected in parallel, we simply add their values together.

$$
C_{eq} = C_1 + C_2
$$

$$
C_{eq} = 4\ \mu\text{F} + 6\ \mu\text{F}
$$

**$C_{eq} = 10\ \mu\text{F}$**

*(In standard SI units: $C_{eq} = 10 \times 10^{-6}\ \text{F}$)*

### Step 2: Calculate the Total Charge Stored ($Q_{total}$)
Using the equivalent capacitance and the given battery voltage, we find the total charge stored in the system.

$$
Q_{total} = C_{eq} \cdot V
$$

$$
Q_{total} = (10 \times 10^{-6}\ \text{F}) \cdot 10\ \text{V}
$$

$$
Q_{total} = 100 \times 10^{-6}\ \text{C}
$$

**$Q_{total} = 100\ \mu\text{C}$**

### Step 3: Calculate the Total Energy Stored ($W_{total}$)
Finally, we calculate the total energy stored using the equivalent capacitance and the square of the voltage.

$$
W_{total} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot C_{eq} \cdot V^2
$$

$$
W_{total} = \frac{1}{2} \cdot (10 \times 10^{-6}\ \text{F}) \cdot (10\ \text{V})^2
$$

$$
W_{total} = 0.5 \cdot (10 \times 10^{-6}) \cdot 100
$$

$$
W_{total} = 500 \times 10^{-6}\ \text{J}
$$

**$W_{total} = 500\ \mu\text{J}$**

---

## Final Results Summary

* **Total Charge Stored ($Q_{total}$):** $100\ \mu\text{C}$
* **Total Energy Stored ($W_{total}$):** $500\ \mu\text{J}$
