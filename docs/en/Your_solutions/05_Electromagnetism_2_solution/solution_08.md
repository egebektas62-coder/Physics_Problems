# Electromagnetic Spectrum Ordering

## Necessary Definitions and Formulas

Before we order the given types of radiation, let's review the fundamental principles that govern the electromagnetic (EM) spectrum.

### 1. The Electromagnetic Spectrum
The electromagnetic spectrum is the range of all types of EM radiation. Radiation is energy that travels and spreads out as it goes. The different types of radiation are distinguished by their wavelength or frequency.

### 2. Wavelength and Frequency Relationship
All electromagnetic waves travel at the speed of light ($c$) in a vacuum. The relationship between the speed of light, wavelength ($\lambda$), and frequency ($f$) is given by:

$$
c = \lambda \cdot f
$$

Because the speed of light $c$ is a constant, **wavelength and frequency are inversely proportional**. This means that radiation with a short wavelength has a high frequency, and radiation with a long wavelength has a low frequency.

### 3. Energy of an EM Wave
The energy ($E$) of a photon of electromagnetic radiation is directly proportional to its frequency and inversely proportional to its wavelength, described by Planck's equation:

$$
E = h \cdot f = \frac{h \cdot c}{\lambda}
$$

*(Where $h$ is Planck's constant). This tells us that shorter wavelengths carry more energy (like Gamma rays), while longer wavelengths carry less energy (like Radio waves).*

## Step-by-Step Solution

**The Goal:** List the following in order of **increasing wavelength** (from shortest to longest): Infrared, Ultraviolet, Microwaves, X-rays, Radio waves, Gamma rays.

### Step 1: Identify the Extremes
*   **Shortest Wavelength (Highest Energy/Frequency):** Gamma rays have the shortest wavelengths in the EM spectrum, often smaller than the size of an atom. They will be first on our list.
*   **Longest Wavelength (Lowest Energy/Frequency):** Radio waves have the longest wavelengths, ranging from about a millimeter to hundreds of kilometers. They will be last on our list.

### Step 2: Order the High-Energy (Short Wavelength) Radiation
Starting from Gamma rays, as we increase the wavelength, we get:
1.  **Gamma rays**
2.  **X-rays:** Slightly longer wavelengths than gamma rays (used in medical imaging).
3.  **Ultraviolet (UV):** Longer than X-rays, but still shorter than visible light (causes sunburns).

### Step 3: Order the Low-Energy (Long Wavelength) Radiation
Continuing past the visible light spectrum (which isn't on our list, but sits between UV and Infrared), we get:
4.  **Infrared (IR):** Longer wavelengths than visible red light (felt as heat).
5.  **Microwaves:** Longer than infrared (used in cooking and radar).
6.  **Radio waves:** The longest wavelengths on the spectrum.

---

## Final Result

Here are the types of electromagnetic radiation listed in order of **increasing wavelength** (shortest to longest):

1.  **Gamma rays**
2.  **X-rays**
3.  **Ultraviolet (UV)**
4.  **Infrared**
5.  **Microwaves**
6.  **Radio waves**
