# Biot-Savart Law: Magnetic Field of a Current Segment

## Necessary Definitions and Formulas

Before diving into the solution, let's review the fundamental principles of the Biot-Savart Law.

### The Biot-Savart Law
The Biot-Savart Law is an equation in electromagnetism that describes the magnetic field generated by a constant electric current. It relates the magnetic field to the magnitude, direction, length, and proximity of the electric current.

For a small segment of wire, the magnitude of the magnetic field $dB$ at a point $P$ is given by the formula:

$$
dB = \frac{\mu_0 \cdot I \cdot dl \cdot \sin(\theta)}{4 \pi \cdot r^2}
$$

Where:
*   **$dB$**: The infinitesimal magnetic field at point $P$ (in Teslas, T).
*   **$\mu_0$**: The vacuum permeability constant ($\mu_0 = 4\pi \times 10^{-7} \text{ T}\cdot\text{m/A}$).
*   **$I$**: The current flowing through the wire (in Amperes, A).
*   **$dl$**: The length of the small current segment (in meters, m).
*   **$r$**: The distance from the current segment to the observation point $P$ (in meters, m).
*   **$\theta$**: The angle between the direction of the current and the line connecting the segment to point $P$.

---

## Step-by-Step Solution

Let's extract the known variables from the problem statement and set up our calculation.

### Step 1: Identify the Known Variables
*   **Current ($I$):** $3 \text{ A}$
*   **Length of the segment ($dl$):** $0.1 \text{ m}$
*   **Distance to point $P$ ($r$):** $0.2 \text{ m}$
*   **Angle ($\theta$):** The problem states the segment is perpendicular to the line connecting it to point $P$. Therefore, the angle is $90^\circ$.

$$
\theta = 90^\circ \implies \sin(90^\circ) = 1
$$

### Step 2: Set Up the Equation
Substitute all the known values into the Biot-Savart magnitude formula.

$$
dB = \frac{(4\pi \times 10^{-7}) \cdot 3 \cdot 0.1 \cdot \sin(90^\circ)}{4\pi \cdot (0.2)^2}
$$

### Step 3: Simplify and Calculate
First, we can cancel out the $4\pi$ terms in the numerator and the denominator, and plug in $1$ for the sine term:

$$
dB = \frac{10^{-7} \cdot 3 \cdot 0.1 \cdot 1}{(0.2)^2}
$$

Next, calculate the products in the numerator and the square in the denominator:

$$
dB = \frac{0.3 \times 10^{-7}}{0.04}
$$

Now, perform the division ($0.3 \div 0.04 = 7.5$):

$$
dB = 7.5 \times 10^{-7} \text{ T}
$$

---

## Final Result

The magnitude of the magnetic field at point $P$ due to the given current segment is **$7.5 \times 10^{-7} \text{ T}$** (which can also be written as **$0.75 \mu\text{T}$**).
