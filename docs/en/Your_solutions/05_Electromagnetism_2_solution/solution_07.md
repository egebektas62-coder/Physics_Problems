# Wavelength and Frequency Analysis

## Necessary Definitions and Formulas

Before solving the problem, here are the essential concepts related to electromagnetic waves and the visible spectrum.

### 1. The Wave Equation for Light
The fundamental relationship between the speed of light ($c$), wavelength ($\lambda$), and frequency ($f$) in a vacuum is given by:

$$
c = \lambda \cdot f
$$

Where:
*   **$c$**: Speed of light in a vacuum ($\approx 3 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$).
*   **$\lambda$**: Wavelength (in meters, m).
*   **$f$**: Frequency (in Hertz, Hz, or $\text{s}^{-1}$).

From this, the formula to find frequency is:

$$
f = \frac{c}{\lambda}
$$

### 2. The Visible Light Spectrum
The human eye can detect electromagnetic radiation in the wavelength range of approximately $380 \text{ nm}$ to $700 \text{ nm}$. 
*   **Violet:** $\sim 380 - 450 \text{ nm}$
*   **Blue:** $\sim 450 - 495 \text{ nm}$
*   **Green:** $\sim 495 - 570 \text{ nm}$
*   **Yellow:** $\sim 570 - 590 \text{ nm}$
*   **Orange:** $\sim 590 - 620 \text{ nm}$
*   **Red:** $\sim 620 - 700 \text{ nm}$

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1: Identify the Known Variables and Convert Units
The given wavelength is $550 \text{ nm}$. We must convert this to the standard SI unit (meters) to use it in our calculations.

$$
\lambda = 550 \text{ nm} = 550 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m}
$$

### Step 2: Determine the Color
Using the standard ranges of the visible light spectrum provided in the definitions above, $550 \text{ nm}$ falls directly into the **Green** range ($495 - 570 \text{ nm}$). This specific wavelength is very close to the peak sensitivity of the human eye under daylight conditions (photopic vision), which appears as a bright, vibrant green (or yellowish-green).

### Step 3: Calculate the Frequency
We use the rearranged wave equation to solve for the frequency $f$.

$$
f = \frac{c}{\lambda}
$$

Substitute the known values ($c = 3 \times 10^8 \text{ m/s}$ and $\lambda = 550 \times 10^{-9} \text{ m}$):

$$
f = \frac{3 \times 10^8}{550 \times 10^{-9}}
$$

Combine the powers of 10 by moving the denominator's exponent to the numerator (which changes its sign to positive):

$$
f = \frac{3}{550} \times 10^{17}
$$

Calculate the division ($3 \div 550 \approx 0.0054545$):

$$
f \approx 0.00545 \times 10^{17} \text{ Hz}
$$

Adjust the scientific notation to standard form by shifting the decimal point two places to the right:

$$
f \approx 5.45 \times 10^{14} \text{ Hz}
$$

> This can also be expressed as:
> $$545 \times 10^{12} \text{ Hz}$$
> *(which is 545 THz or Terahertz).*

---

## Final Results Summary

*   **Color in the visible spectrum:** Green (Yellowish-green)
*   **Frequency of the light ($f$):** $\approx 5.45 \times 10^{14} \text{ Hz}$
