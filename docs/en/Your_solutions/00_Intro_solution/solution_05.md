# 5. Trigonometry: Vector Components

## Necessary Definitions and Formulas

### 1. Vectors and Components
A vector is a quantity that has both magnitude and direction. In a two-dimensional Cartesian coordinate system, any vector can be resolved (broken down) into two perpendicular components: a horizontal component along the x-axis and a vertical component along the y-axis.



### 2. Trigonometric Ratios for Vectors
If a vector $\vec{A}$ has a magnitude $A$ and makes an angle $\theta$ with the positive horizontal axis, its components are given by the following formulas:

* **Horizontal Component ($A_x$)**:

$$
A_x = A \cos(\theta)
$$

* **Vertical Component ($A_y$)**:

$$
A_y = A \sin(\theta)
$$

### 3. Exact Trigonometric Values
For solving this problem exactly, it is helpful to know the standard trigonometric values for $60^\circ$:
* $\cos(60^\circ) = \frac{1}{2}$
* $\sin(60^\circ) = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$

---

## Problem Statement

A vector $\vec{A}$ has a magnitude of $15$ and makes an angle of $\theta = 60^\circ$ with the horizontal axis. Calculate its horizontal and vertical components.

---

## Step-by-Step Solution

### Step 1: Identify the given information
First, we list the known values provided in the problem statement:
* Magnitude $A = 15$
* Angle $\theta = 60^\circ$

### Step 2: Calculate the horizontal component ($A_x$)
We substitute the given magnitude and angle into the horizontal component formula:

$$
A_x = 15 \cos(60^\circ)
$$

Substitute the exact value for $\cos(60^\circ)$:

$$
A_x = 15 \left(\frac{1}{2}\right)
$$

$$
A_x = 7.5
$$

### Step 3: Calculate the vertical component ($A_y$)
Next, we substitute the magnitude and angle into the vertical component formula:

$$
A_y = 15 \sin(60^\circ)
$$

Substitute the exact value for $\sin(60^\circ)$:

$$
A_y = 15 \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)
$$

$$
A_y = 7.5\sqrt{3}
$$

*(If a decimal approximation is needed, $7.5 \times \sqrt{3} \approx 12.99$)*

---

## Final Result

The components of the vector $\vec{A}$ are:
* **Horizontal component ($A_x$)**: $7.5$
* **Vertical component ($A_y$)**: $7.5\sqrt{3}$ (approximately $12.99$)
