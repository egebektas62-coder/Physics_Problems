# Solving a System of Linear Equations

## 1. Necessary Definitions

### System of Equations
A **system of equations** is a set of two or more equations with the same variables that must be satisfied **simultaneously**.

Example:

$$
\begin{cases}
2x + 3y = 12 \\
x - y = 1
\end{cases}
$$

The goal is to find values of the variables (here **x** and **y**) that make **both equations true at the same time**.

---

## 2. Methods to Solve Systems
Common methods include:

- **Substitution Method**
- **Elimination Method**
- **Graphical Method**

In this solution we will use the **substitution method**.

---

## 3. Substitution Method Formula Idea

1. Solve one equation for one variable.
2. Substitute that expression into the other equation.
3. Solve the resulting equation.
4. Substitute back to find the second variable.

---

# Problem

Solve the system:

$$
2x + 3y = 12
$$

$$
x - y = 1
$$

---

# Step 1: Solve the Second Equation for \(x\)

From

$$
x - y = 1
$$

Add \(y\) to both sides:

$$
x = 1 + y
$$

---

# Step 2: Substitute Into the First Equation

Original first equation:

$$
2x + 3y = 12
$$

Substitute \(x = 1 + y\):

$$
2(1 + y) + 3y = 12
$$

---

# Step 3: Simplify the Equation

Distribute the 2:

$$
2 + 2y + 3y = 12
$$

Combine like terms:

$$
2 + 5y = 12
$$

---

# Step 4: Solve for \(y\)

Subtract 2 from both sides:

$$
5y = 10
$$

Divide by 5:

$$
y = 2
$$

---

# Step 5: Substitute Back to Find \(x\)

Recall:

$$
x = 1 + y
$$

Substitute \(y = 2\):

$$
x = 1 + 2
$$

$$
x = 3
$$

---

# Step 6: Final Solution

The values that satisfy both equations are:

$$
x = 3
$$

$$
y = 2
$$

---

# Step 7: Verification (Optional but Recommended)

Check in the first equation:

$$
2x + 3y = 12
$$

Substitute \(x=3\), \(y=2\):

$$
2(3) + 3(2) = 6 + 6 = 12
$$

✔ Correct.

Check the second equation:

$$
x - y = 1
$$

$$
3 - 2 = 1
$$

✔ Correct.

---

# Final Answer

$$
(x, y) = (3, 2)
$$

Both equations are satisfied by **x = 3** and **y = 2**.
